## Executive overview

The Credits.com Outbound GTM project defined how a small commercial team could develop a more structured approach to prospecting and partner-led acquisition.

The work covered audience segmentation, sourcing channels, lead-record requirements, messaging sequences, and referral operations. Its purpose was to establish a repeatable process before expanding automation.

The supplied document is a historical blueprint. It should not be read as a current description of the company's CRM, commercial terms, market eligibility, or technology stack.

## Operational problem

Increasing outreach volume without consistent records and responsibilities can create more activity without a clearer pipeline.

The blueprint addressed the basic operating requirements: which prospects to target, what information to capture, how to tailor the message, and when responsibility moves to the next step.

## Documented scope

The manual phase defined sourcing through professional networks, registries, industry groups, events, and referral relationships.

Lead records were intended to capture vertical, product interest, status, and follow-up. Messaging was organized by audience segment rather than relying on a single broad sequence.

Referral workflows added a separate route for qualified introductions and pre-referral review.

## Automation roadmap

The historical plan proposed tools for sourcing, enrichment, outbound email, CRM management, and alerts. Some were deferred or not yet activated.

There is a real distinction between designing this architecture and having every integration operational — later work may have superseded the original plan. A current CRM or Revenue OS would be documented as a separate build or a clearly dated evolution, using its own evidence, not folded into this historical blueprint.

## What this demonstrates

Commercial process design that connects targeting, activity, ownership, and follow-up before scaling automation.
